Coloropolis is a coloring app for children on iPhone and iPad: 200 pictures on 10 planets, with no
ads, no purchases, no accounts and no internet connection.

## Report a problem

Please include the device model, the iOS or iPadOS version, the app version (shown on the App Store
page) and what happened. Tell us which picture it was, if it matters. A screenshot helps, but please
remove any child's name first, and don't send photos of children.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does a child find a picture?

Swipe the galaxy to choose a planet (Animals, Vehicles, Fruits & Veggies, Dinosaurs, Royal world,
Town & buildings, Nature & garden, Music, Sports & play, Everyday objects) and tap it. Then spin the
planet and tap a picture.

### What can they color with?

**Crayon**, **Marker**, **Fill** (tap an area to fill it), **Eraser** and **Stickers**. The paint box
has plain **Colors** and three special paints: **Glow**, **Glitter** and **Shimmer**. Paint stays
inside the lines. **Undo** takes back the last step, and **Full screen** hides the tools.

The **More** button (•••) has **Lamp** (lights off, so glow paint shines), **Mirror** (paint both sides
at once), **Numbers** (color by number), **Notes** (every crayon stroke plays a note) and **Start
over**.

### What happens when a picture is finished?

It comes alive with a little celebration. **Watch it** replays the picture painting itself. Every
picture is kept in **My pictures** on the home screen; finished ones wear a star.

### How do we save, print or share a picture?

In the picture, tap **Keep it**. After a short grown-up check you can choose **Save to Photos**,
**Print or share**, **Print blank page to color** or **Watch the replay**.

### It says "Photos access is off". What now?

The app needs permission to add pictures to Photos. Open the **Settings** app → **Coloropolis** →
**Photos** and allow adding photos, then try again.

### Can brothers and sisters share one iPad?

Yes. Tap the profile at the top of the home screen ("Who's coloring?") to switch, or add a **New
profile** with a name, an emoji and a color. Each child has their own pictures. To edit a profile, tap
its pencil. Deleting a profile (the trash button, behind the grown-up check) removes that child's
pictures and can't be undone.

### How do we turn music or sounds off?

Tap the **gear** on the home screen to open **Parents**, and switch **Music** or **Sound effects** off.
The first profile also asks whether to turn sounds on. Check the device volume and the silent switch
too.

### How do we export all the pictures?

Tap the **gear** on the home screen, then **Export all pictures**. After the grown-up check, the share
screen lets you save or send them all.

### Does it work offline? Is there a subscription?

It works completely offline and never connects to the internet. There are no ads, subscriptions or
in-app purchases.

### Which languages does it speak?

English. From version 1.2 it also speaks Ukrainian and Russian.

### Where are the pictures stored?

Only on the device. Deleting the app deletes them, so save or export the ones you want to keep first.
See the [Privacy Policy](PrivacyPolicy.md).

### Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, in portrait and landscape.
