# Coloropolis — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

_Also available in: [Українська](PrivacyPolicy_ua.md) · [Русский](PrivacyPolicy_ru.md)_

> **The short version:** Coloropolis collects nothing. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no
> in-app purchases, and it doesn't connect to the internet. Everything a child makes stays on the device.

## Who we are

Coloropolis is a coloring app for children on iPhone and iPad, published by **Oneekz Productions LLC**
(Magnolia, Texas, USA; "we"). This policy explains how the app handles information, and the answer is
that it doesn't collect any.

## Information we collect

**None.** Coloropolis does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no
sign-up, no login and no server. The app has no networking code at all, so we never receive anything
from it.

## What stays on the device

To make the app work, the following is saved **only on your device** and is never sent to us or anyone
else:

- **The pictures your child colors**, finished or in progress, and the stickers placed on them.
- **Child profiles**, so that brothers and sisters can each keep their own pictures. A profile is a
  name your child types in (it doesn't have to be a real name), an emoji and an avatar color. Profiles
  are not online accounts and are not linked to any real identity.
- **Simple settings**, such as the last crayon and planet, which profile is active, and whether music
  and sound effects are on.

Deleting a profile removes that child's pictures. Deleting the app removes everything. Your device's
own backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data, according to your device settings; that is
handled by Apple, not by us.

## Photos, printing and sharing

If a grown-up chooses **Save to Photos**, the app asks for permission to **add** that one picture to
the Photos library. It never reads or browses your existing photos. Printing and sharing use Apple's
standard print and share screens; what happens after that depends on the printer, app or service you
choose.

## Children's privacy

Coloropolis is made for children. It collects no data,
shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and contains no links out of the app. It does not knowingly
gather information from children or anyone else, which keeps it in line with the U.S. Children's
Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar
laws.

Everything meant for a grown-up (saving, printing or sharing a picture, exporting all pictures, and
deleting a profile) sits behind a parental gate.

## Third parties

There are no third-party advertisers, analytics providers or SDKs of any kind. The app is built
entirely with Apple's own frameworks.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
