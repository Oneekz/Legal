# Privacy Policy — Daily Word Lamp

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

_Also available in: [Українська](https://github.com/Oneekz/Legal/blob/main/Daily%20Word%20Lamp/PrivacyPolicy_ua.md) · [Русский](https://github.com/Oneekz/Legal/blob/main/Daily%20Word%20Lamp/PrivacyPolicy_ru.md) · [Polski](https://github.com/Oneekz/Legal/blob/main/Daily%20Word%20Lamp/PrivacyPolicy_pl.md)_

Daily Word Lamp helps you read the Bible every day. To keep your reading in step
across your devices and share it with your fellowship, it needs an account. This
policy explains what that account holds, who can see it, and how to delete it.

## Who we are
Daily Word Lamp is developed and published by **Oneekz Productions LLC**, based in
Magnolia, Texas, USA (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the app on iPhone and iPad
(App Store), the app on Android (Google Play) and the website
[dailywordlamp.oneekz.com](https://dailywordlamp.oneekz.com).

## In short
- We keep only what the app needs to work: your account, your reading log and
  what you choose to share with others.
- **No advertising, no analytics, no tracking**, and we never sell or rent your data.
- The Bible texts are built into the app; reading them happens on your device.
- You can export your data or delete your account at any time in Settings.

## What we keep
**Your account**
- your name, email address and password. The password is stored only as a
  one-way hash; we can't read it;
- a profile photo, if you add one;
- your preferences: interface language, time zone, start page, who can see your
  readings, your primary fellowship, and the reminder hour if you turn the daily
  reminder on.

**Your reading**
- the chapters you mark as read and the days you read them, and the streaks and
  progress worked out from them;
- saved places (the verse you stopped at in a chapter);
- reading plans you follow or create.

**Reading together**
- the fellowships you create or join, join requests, and fellowship invite codes;
- the people you follow, and the hearts you give to others' readings;
- your public progress page, only if you turn it on. It shows your name, your
  percentage of the Bible, your streak and completions, and nothing else.

**Technical data**
- when you sign in, a session record with your IP address and the type of device
  or browser, so you stay signed in and can sign out. A session lasts up to 7 days
  after you last use it, or until you sign out;
- on iPhone, if you allow notifications, a device notification token. We store it
  but send nothing to it: the daily reminder is scheduled on your phone itself;
- on the website, one sign-in cookie. There are no advertising or analytics
  cookies.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, or any data from other apps. The
app shows your photo library only when you choose a profile photo, and only the
photo you pick is uploaded.

## Who can see it
- **Your readings:** you choose in Settings ▸ Privacy: everyone, your fellowship,
  or only you.
- **Your fellowship** sees your name, photo, progress and streak in the member list.
- **Your public progress page** can be seen by anyone with its link, only while it's on.
- **We** can see the data on our server to run and support the service.

## Services that help us run it
- **Our server**, which we rent and run ourselves, stores the data above. It is
  backed up every day; backups stay on our own systems.
- **Cloudflare** sits in front of the server to keep it fast and protect it from
  attacks. It handles your connection, including your IP address.
- **Resend** sends the account emails (welcome and password reset). It receives
  your email address and that message.
- **Apple** and **Google** deliver the apps and app updates under their own
  privacy policies.

Cloudflare and Resend handle your data only to provide their service to us.

## Keeping and deleting your data
We keep your data while your account exists.
- **Export:** Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Export my data gives you a copy of your data.
- **Delete:** Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Delete account. After a 24-hour cooling-off
  period (you can cancel during it), your account, reading log, photo and
  everything linked to it are deleted from the live service. Copies can remain in
  our daily server backups until those backups are removed.
- You can also email us to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion.

## Children
Daily Word Lamp is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account,
contact us and we'll delete it.

## The Bible texts
The King James Version, the Ohienko translation, the Synodal translation and the
Biblia Gdańska are bundled with the app and used under their licences; the sources
are listed in Settings ▸ Bible translation ▸ About this translation. Reading them
sends nothing to anyone. Only what you mark as read or save is sent to your account.
