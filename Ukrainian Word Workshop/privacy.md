# Privacy Policy — Зоряна майстерня слів

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

_Also available in: [Українська](privacy.uk.md)_

Зоряна майстерня слів (displayed on the device as «Майстерня слів», and described in English as Ukrainian Word Workshop) is a game for learning to read Ukrainian on iPhone and iPad. Its interface can be English or Ukrainian; the reading material and all recordings are Ukrainian. The publisher is **Oneekz Productions LLC**.

**We do not receive personal data from the app. Learning progress is stored locally. There are no online accounts, ads, tracking or in-app purchases.**

## Local learning data

The app stores completed lessons, collected words, reading positions, game progress, ship rewards and settings on the device so that play can continue later. Settings include the interface language (System default, English or Ukrainian), which applies to everyone on that device and is not sent anywhere.

Each local player profile has a chosen nickname, a built-in avatar and separate learning progress; these are also stored on that device. A nickname does not need to be a child's real name. Local profiles are not online accounts and are not sent to us. We do not ask for children's email addresses, birth dates, photographs or contact details.

## Audio and permissions

Learning recordings and illustrations are included with the app. All recordings are Ukrainian; the English interface, word meanings and translations are text included with the app, not generated or fetched online. Playing an exercise does not record the child, upload their voice or contact a speech-generation service. The app does not request microphone, camera, contacts or location access for learning activities.

## Services and tracking

The app does not use advertising, analytics, attribution, third-party crash-reporting or social-login services. It does not use advertising identifiers, run a developer-operated account service or synchronize progress through a developer-operated server. It does not sell or share learning data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Device backups

The app saves progress locally and does not provide its own cloud-sync service. Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include app data in a device backup or transfer it when you move to another device. Those backups are managed by you and the platform provider; we do not receive or control them. See [Apple's privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The reading activities are designed with younger learners in mind. We do not receive children's learning records or build online profiles about them. Adults can use nicknames and built-in avatars rather than identifying information.

## Deleting local data

Use the progress-reset controls in the adult area to erase learning progress on the device. Deleting a player profile removes that profile's local learning data. Removing the app and its data also removes its local records; offloading an app while keeping its data is different from deleting that data. A device backup may retain an earlier copy according to your backup settings.

Because we do not hold the app's learning records, we cannot restore or remotely delete them. For backup management, use the device's own settings.

## Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to answer your request and resolve the issue, retaining it as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Do not include a child's real name, photograph or other unnecessary identifying information. You can ask us to delete a support conversation by writing to the same address.

Email is handled by the email providers involved in delivery. Sending an email is separate from the app's local learning storage.

## External pages

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub. Opening those pages or an email link uses an external service, subject to that service's own privacy practices. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). In the app, these links are in For grown-ups → Privacy and support («Для дорослих» → «Конфіденційність і підтримка») and open only after a question meant for adults. They open the English or Ukrainian page, matching the interface language. Learning in the app does not require opening those pages.
