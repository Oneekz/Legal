_Also available in: [Українська](support.uk.md)_

**Oneekz Productions LLC**  
Write to **oneekz.productions [at] gmail [dot] com** in Ukrainian or English.

The app's interface can be English or Ukrainian. What the child reads (syllable blocks, words, sentences and the story) and all recorded voices are Ukrainian.

## Report a problem

Please include the device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version and what happened. For a learning-content issue, include the word, lesson, sentence or story line. The same applies to an English word, meaning or translation that seems wrong. A screenshot can help, but remove any child's name or other identifying information first. Do not send children's photos or voice recordings.

If a sound seems wrong, tell us whether it is a block sound, the whole word or the slower recording. Those can be different audio files.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it work offline?

Learning content, illustrations and audio are included in the app. After installation, learning activities do not require an internet connection. Opening a support/privacy webpage or sending email uses the services involved in that action.

### Can the app be used in English?

Yes. Open For grown-ups and choose a language on the «Мова · Language» card: System default, English or Українська. System default uses the first of Ukrainian or English in your device's language list, and English if neither is listed. The choice applies at once, to everyone on the device, and doesn't change anyone's progress.

In English, the buttons, instructions and help are English text. Each word picture shows its English word and opens a short meaning, and sentences and story lines have English translations. The reading itself stays Ukrainian.

### Is there English audio?

No. All recordings are Ukrainian: syllables, words, sentences, the story and the guide. In English, the guide's Ukrainian speech is shown as a short English line on screen.

### Is there a subscription or purchase?

No. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

### Where is progress saved?

Progress is stored locally on the device. There is no developer-operated account or cloud-sync service. Device backups may include app data according to your operating-system settings; this is not a cross-platform sync feature.

### Can siblings use one iPad?

Yes. Tap the player name at the top (“Who's playing?” / «Хто грає?») and choose or create each child's own player before starting. Each profile has separate progress. Profile nicknames and built-in avatars stay on the device; no online registration is needed.

### Can we play without dragging?

Yes. Enable “No dragging” («Без перетягування») in a lesson, Help, Pause or the adult settings. Tap a block to choose it, then tap the next empty socket. Turn the option off to restore dragging.

### How do we reset progress?

Use the reset controls in For grown-ups («Для батьків»). Resetting is destructive and asks for confirmation. Check the selected player before resetting or deleting their data; other players are not affected.

### Why are some lessons locked?

Later lessons open as earlier lessons are completed. The level and lesson cards explain what opens next.

### Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.

### Where are the privacy and support links in the app?

In For grown-ups → Privacy and support («Для батьків» → «Конфіденційність і підтримка»). The links open in the browser or the mail app only after a question meant for adults.
