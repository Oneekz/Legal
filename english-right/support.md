# English Right — Support

Write to **oneekz.productions [at] gmail [dot] com**. Messages in Ukrainian or English are
both fine, and you usually get an answer within a few days.

To make a bug report useful, include: your iPhone model, the iOS version, the app version
(profile screen, at the very bottom) and a screenshot if you can.

> The app itself is in Ukrainian — it teaches English *to Ukrainian speakers*, so the
> interface, the explanations and the translations are all Ukrainian. The material being
> taught is English.

## Frequently asked questions

### I paid, but the levels are locked (new phone, reinstall)

Open the premium screen — from any locked level card on the home screen, or from the profile
screen — and tap the restore-purchase button below the price. The purchase is tied to your
Apple Account, so it comes back at no cost on any device signed in to that account.

### The level downloaded, but the units still show a padlock

That's the course structure, not a fault. A level opens once you pass the previous level's
consolidation review, the summary test at the end of that level. If you already know the
material, take the test-out on a unit and skip ahead immediately.

### The download fails or stops halfway

Each level is roughly 50–65 MB. Check that you have a working connection and enough free
space, then tap the retry button on the level card. Downloads resume rather than start over.
If it keeps failing, send us the exact error message shown on the card.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes. The first level works offline from the moment you install the app. Every other level
works offline once it has been downloaded — audio, images and exercises are all stored on the
device. You only need a connection for the download itself.

### Will my progress survive a new phone?

If you are signed in to iCloud, progress and flashcard scheduling sync through your own iCloud
account automatically. If you don't use iCloud, use the backup item in the profile screen to
save a `.json` file, and restore it on the new device.

### I don't want anything in iCloud

The profile screen has an iCloud sync switch — turn it off. Syncing stops and the copy already
in iCloud is erased; your progress on this device stays exactly as it is. Do make a manual
backup afterwards — without iCloud, that file is your only safety net.

### The pronunciation exercises don't hear me

They need microphone permission, and speech recognition permission for some exercises. Check
iOS Settings → English Right. Speech recognition runs entirely on your device, so an older
device without on-device recognition for English will skip those exercises.

### I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: go to
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in with your Apple Account
and choose the purchase.

### I found a mistake in the English (or in a translation)

Please tell us — those reports are genuinely valuable. Include the level, the unit and, if you
can, a screenshot. Content fixes ship in ordinary app updates.
