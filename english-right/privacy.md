# Privacy Policy — English Right

**Last updated: 5 August 2026**

English Right ("the app") is an English course for Ukrainian speakers, published by
Yurii Kashuba.

**The short version: the app collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no sign-up,
no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. There is no third-party SDK in the
app at all.

## 1. What stays on your device

Everything you produce while learning is stored locally on your iPhone:

- lesson and unit progress, test results, streaks and achievements
- flashcard scheduling data for spaced repetition
- your mistake journal, notes and any cards you create yourself
- app settings

This data never leaves your device except through the two mechanisms described below, both of
which you control.

## 2. iCloud sync — your choice

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app mirrors a small amount of that progress data
(a few hundred kilobytes of JSON) through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it survives a
reinstall or a new phone. That copy lives in **your own iCloud account**. We have no access to
it, cannot read it and never receive it. Its handling is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

It is on by default, because losing a year of study along with a phone is the worse outcome —
but it is yours to switch off. The profile screen has an iCloud sync switch: turning it off
stops the syncing and erases the copy already held in iCloud. Your progress on the device is
untouched; it simply stops leaving it. (Turning iCloud Drive off for English Right in iOS
Settings, or signing out of iCloud, also stops the sync.)

The app can also write a manual backup to a plain `.json` file that you choose to save or
send. That file goes wherever you send it — we never see it.

## 3. Microphone and speech recognition

The pronunciation exercises ask for microphone permission, and optionally for speech
recognition. Both are entirely optional; the rest of the app works without them.

- Recorded audio is held in memory only for the few seconds needed to score it. It is never
  written to storage and never uploaded.
- Speech recognition runs **on-device only** (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition`). The app refuses
  to run the exercise at all rather than send audio to a server.
- We receive no audio, no transcripts and no pronunciation scores.

You can revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings → English Right.

## 4. Purchases

The premium levels are a one-time in-app purchase. **Apple processes the payment.** We never
see your name, email address, card details or billing address.

To unlock the levels you bought, the app sends Apple's cryptographically signed transaction to
our content server at `lang.dailywordlamp.com`. That transaction contains a transaction
identifier, the product identifier, the app's bundle identifier and the purchase date — it
does not contain your name, your email address or your Apple Account. Our server verifies it
with Apple's App Store Server API and then allows the download. It does not store a customer
record for you.

## 5. Our content server

When the app downloads a level from `lang.dailywordlamp.com`, that server sees your device's
IP address, exactly as any web server does when you visit a page. It is used to deliver the
download and to rate-limit abuse. It is not used to build a profile, is not linked to your
purchase and is not shared. Standard access logs are kept only briefly, for security and
diagnostics, and are then discarded.

The server holds no user accounts and no database of users.

## 6. What we never do

- We do not sell, rent or share your data — there is nothing to sell.
- We show no advertising and use no advertising identifiers.
- We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app never asks to.
- We use no analytics, crash-reporting or attribution service.

## 7. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. Because it collects no personal data from
anyone, it collects none from children either.

## 8. Deleting your data

Delete the app and the on-device data goes with it. To remove the iCloud copy as well, turn
off iCloud Drive for English Right in iOS Settings before deleting, or delete the app's data
from iCloud settings afterwards. Since we hold no personal data on our side, there is nothing
for us to delete on request — but you are welcome to write and ask.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Ukrainian data-protection law you have the right to access, correct,
export or erase personal data held about you. We hold none, so in practice these rights are
satisfied by the paragraph above. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will answer.
