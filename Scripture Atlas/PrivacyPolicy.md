# Privacy Policy — Scripture Atlas

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

_Also available in: [Українська](https://github.com/Oneekz/Legal/blob/main/Scripture%20Atlas/PrivacyPolicy_ua.md) · [Русский](https://github.com/Oneekz/Legal/blob/main/Scripture%20Atlas/PrivacyPolicy_ru.md)_

Scripture Atlas is designed to protect your privacy completely.

## Who we are
Scripture Atlas is developed and published by **Oneekz Productions LLC**, based in
Magnolia, Texas, USA (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the app on iPhone and iPad
(App Store) and on Android (Google Play).

## We collect nothing
Scripture Atlas does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data. Nothing about you ever reaches us, so there is nothing for us to keep or
delete. The app:

- makes **no network requests** — all maps and data are bundled and work fully offline (on Android, the app doesn't even have permission to access the internet);
- contains **no analytics, advertising, or tracking** of any kind;
- requires **no account** and asks for **no permissions** (no location, contacts, camera, or photos).

## What stays on your device
The only things the app stores are your **settings** — your chosen interface
language, distance units, the Bible / Extended mode toggle, and which layers and
tools are shown on the map — the places you add to **Favorites** or viewed
recently, and where you left off (the era, map view and passage you were
reading). They are saved locally on your device, in the standard preferences
storage of iOS or Android, and the app never sends them anywhere. If your
device's own backup is turned on (iCloud or a
computer backup on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android), they may be
included in it — a backup you control, not us. They are deleted from your device
when you delete the app.

## Children
Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of any age.

## Third-party data
The maps and information shown are built from openly licensed data (OpenBible.info,
Natural Earth) and public reference sources, credited inside the app under
“Sources & credits.” These providers receive no information about you — the app
never contacts them.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.
