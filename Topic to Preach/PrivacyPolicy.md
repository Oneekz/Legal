### In short

**Topic to Preach** collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no network request, no analytics and no advertising. The app runs entirely offline.

### Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from you — not personal data, not anonymised data, not usage statistics.

### What is stored on your device

Everything you create in the app stays on your device only:

- bookmarked topics;
- your private notes on topics;
- settings: language, appearance (light/dark), Old Testament visibility, church-year rules.

This is held in the app's standard iOS preferences storage. It is never transmitted. If you have iCloud or iTunes backup enabled it may be included in your own backup — a backup you control, not the developer.

Deleting the app erases all of it.

### Sharing with third parties

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, ad networks or analytics tools.

### Children

The app collects no data, and therefore collects no data from children.

### Texts in the app

The Bible and reference texts are bundled with the app and used under their respective licences. Attribution is shown in Settings → Text sources.
